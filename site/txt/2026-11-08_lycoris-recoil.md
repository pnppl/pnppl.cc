# Lycoris Recoil
#anime #tv #watched-2026 #review

## The fuck?
The premise is really, really, really dumb; it's clearly a flimsy excuse to have gun-toting high school girls plus slice-of-life chill.

At first I thought it was a send-up of nationalism. Child soldiers who extrajudicially assassinate bad guys for the state? But actually I think we're just supposed to accept that part. The *bad guy* is trying to expose them... and also doing terrorism.

## Pedos welcome!
Did we really need an episode about their panties? Why did they have to be high schoolers? Why does every anime series treat pedophiles like a key audience demographic?

It doesn't really sexualize them, at least, even in the panty episode. It's actually quite good by anime standards, but the bar is on the floor. I'm so fucking tired of anime's obsession with high school.

## MAKE IT GAYER
The fanbase seems to be bifurcated on the question of whether it's queerbait and how overt the subtext is. I don't have a strong opinion either way, but the lack of explicit romance feels like such a missed opportunity. The emotional core of the series is hollowed out by the refusal to make it gayer. The conclusion feels so needlessly empty.

There is an explicitly gay black character who isn't a stereotype, which was refreshing.

## 3DPD
There's a lot of CGI in here that just looks really ugly to me. It blends in with the traditional style better than some series I've seen, though.

## Hackers wear masks 24/7?
Why not just turn off the webcam?

## Girl Vash
The best part of the series is Chisato, a bubbly gun-toting pacifist. She shoots some kind of paintball bullets instead of real ones. (And is somehow still on the secret police payroll in spite of this?)

It's very dumb, but in a fun and wholesome way. She reminded me a lot of Vash the Stampede from _Trigun_. Like Vash, she has a superhuman ability to dodge bullets. Her story arc is pretty much the same, too, but with a weird abusive quasi-adoptive quasi-parent playing the role of Knives.

## Verdict
It starts out bad, gets better, then fades back into meh. If you're looking for silly _John Wick_/_Equilibrium_-style action you'll probably enjoy it, but if you're looking for characters and drama you'll be disappointed.

**3/5** (rounded up because [[the music slaps|https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Od3ifGNDnwY]])
