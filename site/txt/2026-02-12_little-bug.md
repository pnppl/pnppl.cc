# little bug
#poem

little bug,
i'm so sorry i hurt you.
it was an accident.
i didn't mean to.

little bug,
i have so many reasons to cry.
i don't want another.
please don't die.

little bug,
was your little life good?
did you eat green leaves,
and do everything you thought you would?

little bug,
we are alike, you and i,
two specks in a land of careless giants.
no one will notice when i die.

little bug,
i can't fix your wings or your leg.
all i can do is notice you.
if god were real i would beg.
