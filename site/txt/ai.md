# Text
Everything you read here is a one hundred percent unintermediated product of my own mind. I typically don't even use a spellcheck. I type emdashes and other exotic punctuation with a [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compose_key|compose key]] or by long-pressing my phone keyboard.

# Audiovisual Media
It's not totally outside the realm of possibility that I would post audio or an image that makes use of the technology, but I would mention it if so. Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, etc., are a lot of fun, but they're something I mess with once in a blue moon. Like Wii Bowling or DDR.

# Code
I consult chatbots for help with the software that builds the site sometimes. I want to learn how it works, so I don't let them touch the code, and I don't generally copy anything they say verbatim; when I do, I add a comment for my own benefit. However, I did make extensive use of the exe.dev system to pare down my CSS, for which I am eternally grateful to them. I also experimented with vibecoding on the same platform until they cut me off. I might post some of that here, but again, I would clearly note the provenance.
