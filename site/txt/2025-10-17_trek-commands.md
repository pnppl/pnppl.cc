# Ten Commandments of Good Star Trek
#star-trek #list

1. Thou shalt not useth time travel
2. Thou shalt not useth fucking time travel
3. Thou shalt not be blatantly sexist
4. Thou shalt put thine female characters in normal uniforms
5. Thou shalt not resolve conflict with brute force
6. Thou shalt not construct elaborate scenarios to get around commandment #5
7. Thou shalt pose genuine moral dilemmas
8. Thou shalt not resolve conflict with technobabble
9. Thou shalt not get cute with thine references to past series unless thou art writing a comedy episode
10. THOU SHALL BE STRUCK DOWN IF THOU USETH TIME TRAVEL
